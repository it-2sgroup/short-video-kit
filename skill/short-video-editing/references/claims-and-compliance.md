# Claims and Compliance — what the words and pictures may say, by category

Read before writing any voiceover or on-screen text that sells something. Most moderation risk lives in
the words, so it is cheaper to get the script right than to re-shoot. Each playbook prints its own
checklist into the plan; this file holds the rules behind those lines.

- **[P]** platform or regulator documentation (TikTok, Meta, FTC): act on it.
- **[L]** Vietnamese law, from research of the texts and legal press: not legal advice; the user's
  lawyer decides close calls.
- **[C]** craft convention.

Originality, reuse, duplicate content and footage rights are in `policy.md`.

## 1. Rules for every category

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Ads are labelled; creators vouch only for what they used** | The amended Advertising Law (Luật Quảng cáo) 75/2025/QH15 (in force 01/01/2026), Article 15a (Điều 15a): KOLs/KOCs must check the advertiser and the product documents and must not promote a product they have not used or do not understand; ads carry a clear identifier before and during the ad; required warnings are legible (contrasting colour, a font no smaller than the rest); violating content comes down within 24 h of an authority's request. The exact on-video label format was not found. | [L] LuatVietnam summaries |
| **Superlatives need documents** | "nhất" (the most), "duy nhất" (the only), "tốt nhất" (the best), "số một" (number one) are banned without legal supporting documents (Advertising Law 2012, Article 8 clause 11; TT 10/2013/TT-BVHTTDL Đ2). The one claim TikTok explicitly permits: your **own** sales ("bán chạy nhất shop mình", the best seller in our own shop). | [L], [P] seller-uk `knowledge_id=5434012677392150` |
| **No competitor comparison** | Content must not compare against other products or sellers, claim to be cheaper, or judge competitors negatively. | [P] seller-th, ads.tiktok.com |
| **Content matches the listing** | Prices, discounts, promotions, sizes stated in the video match the destination listing. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| **No false scarcity or urgency** | "Chỉ còn 3 suất" (only 3 spots left) only when real and reflected on the listing. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| **No fake interactive elements** | No fake play, close, CTA or cart buttons: point at the real product bar. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| **Commercial disclosure** | Turn on "Disclose commercial content" whenever anything of value changed hands; undisclosed content loses visibility, repeat violations escalate. | [P] tiktok.com community guidelines |
| **AI-edited content disclosed** | Substantially AI-generated or AI-edited content is labelled; AI demos showing instant or unrealistic effects are prohibited. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| **Guaranteed results / money-back** | Exaggerated, unverifiable promises are prohibited ("không hiệu quả hoàn tiền", money back if it does not work). | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| **Miracle framing** | "Công thức bí mật" (secret formula), "đột phá" (breakthrough), "thần kỳ" (miraculous) are named as exaggerated promises. | [P] seller-th |

**The useful insight.** The things that make a demonstration believable (an unbroken take, a locked
frame, constant light, visible hands, an honestly imperfect result) are the things that make it
compliant. Specific, bounded, first-person observations replace absolute claims, and specificity is
also what performs better (`niche-winning-patterns.md` §5).

## 2. Household and cleaning products (`product-demo`)

| Prohibited | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Antibacterial / antimicrobial | Cleaning products may not claim to kill or inhibit bacteria, fungi, viruses; allowed: cleaning dirt while *reducing the accumulation* of bacteria. | [P] seller-th `knowledge_id=4924400659187458` |
| Instant effect | "Sạch ngay lập tức" (clean instantly), "instant removal/restoration". | [P] same; seller-us `knowledge_id=3196690250417921` |
| Ends all problems | "Sạch mọi vết bẩn" (cleans every stain), "removes any stain". | [P] seller-th |

Rewrite table (keep the persuasion, lose the claim; the Vietnamese wordings are real examples that moderators flag, the English meaning is in parentheses):

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Diệt khuẩn 99.9%" (kills 99.9% of bacteria) | "Làm sạch bụi bẩn bám trên vải" (cleans the dirt stuck on the fabric) |
| "Tẩy sạch ngay lập tức" (removes it instantly) | "Ngâm 2 tiếng là thấy khác rõ" (soak for 2 hours and the difference is clear) |
| "Sạch mọi vết bẩn" (cleans every stain) | "Mình thử với vài loại vết cụ thể (nói rõ từng loại)" (I tried a few specific kinds of stain; name each kind) |
| "Trắng bóc như mới" (spotless white like new) | "Sáng hơn hẳn so với lúc đầu" (much brighter than at the start) |
| "Không sạch hoàn tiền" (money back if not clean) | *(remove; no compliant rewrite)* |
| "Tốt nhất thị trường" (best on the market) | "Sản phẩm bán chạy nhất shop mình trên TikTok Shop" (the best-selling product of our shop on TikTok Shop) |
| "Hơn hẳn mấy loại khác" (far better than the others) | "So với cách mình giặt tay trước đây thì đỡ công hơn" (compared with how I used to wash by hand it takes less effort) |
| "Công thức bí mật" (secret formula) | "Thành phần chính ghi trên bao bì (nêu đúng tên)" (the main ingredient printed on the packaging; name it exactly) |
| "Chỉ còn 3 suất cuối" (only the last 3 spots) | *(remove unless the listing shows limited stock)* |

## 3. Before/after demonstrations (any category)

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No false impression | Before/after creating a false impression of outcomes is prohibited ("problematic comparisons"). | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| Same lighting, angle, editing | Different light, angle or editing between before and after is a misleading claim. **The same grade and the same correction** on both (`prepare_footage.py` with `correction: shared`). | [P] seller-us `knowledge_id=4581457528194817` |
| Real performance, typical results | A demonstration reflects performance under normal use; results are typical or the expected result is disclosed. | [P] FTC guides |
| Time compression labelled | "tua nhanh ×8" (sped up ×8), "sau 2 tiếng" (after 2 hours) on screen. | [P] instant-effect rule; FTC |
| **Ads: none** | TikTok ads and landing pages must not include before-and-after product effect comparisons. If the video will be boosted, make a version without the `compare` card and BEFORE/AFTER labels. | [P] ads.tiktok.com "Misleading and false content" |
| Banned categories | Before/after is banned outright for acne, whitening and anti-hair-loss products. | [P] TikTok Shop listing rules |

How to build a credible one: `before-after.md`.

## 4. Real estate (`real-estate`)

- **Real Estate Business Law 2023** (Luật Kinh doanh bất động sản, 29/2023/QH15, in force 01/08/2024) [L]: Article 6 — before
  selling, the business publishes full, truthful, accurate information on the national system and its
  own website (approvals, land decisions, planning, contract forms; for future housing the permits, the
  sale guarantee and the authority's confirmation that it may be sold; for existing housing the
  ownership certificate; mortgages and restrictions). Article 8 bans doing business without meeting the
  conditions, forging or distorting information, not disclosing it, fraud, and collecting money
  improperly for future housing. Advertising itself falls under the Advertising Law.
- **Penalties**: Decree 339/2026/NĐ-CP (in force 26/08/2026, replaces Decree 16/2022): up to 1 billion VND; selling
  housing that does not meet the conditions 800 million–1 billion VND; unlicensed broker 40–60 million VND (press summaries).
- **What the edit does**: facts (price, area measured as net internal (thông thủy) or wall-centre (tim tường), rooms, direction, legal status)
  exactly as the documents say, never rounded or invented; for an off-plan project, ask whether it may
  be sold; renders and virtual staging labelled "hình minh hoạ" (illustration); travel times labelled as estimates; no
  promised returns, yields or price rises; broker identified; neighbours, faces and other houses'
  numbers out of frame.
- TikTok does not restrict real-estate ads in Vietnam; licensed advertiser and advertiser details on
  the landing page [P].

## 5. Food, drinks, supplements (`food-fnb`)

- Ordinary food is not medicine: no "chữa" (cure), "trị" (treat), "giảm cân" (lose weight), "tốt cho gan" (good for the liver) [L].
- Health-supplement food (TPBVSK, thực phẩm bảo vệ sức khỏe) ads need registered ad content and the warning *"Thực phẩm này không
  phải là thuốc, không có tác dụng thay thế thuốc chữa bệnh"* ("This food is not a medicine and does not replace medicine for treating disease"), shown or read clearly; no doctors or
  medical staff in food or TPBVSK ads [L] (Decree 15/2018/NĐ-CP Article 27, penalties in Decree 38/2021; check the current text).
- Alcohol: no appeal to minors, no drinking and driving, platform alcohol rules; ask the user [P].
- Prices as on the menu that day; promotions with dates.

## 6. Cosmetics and beauty (`fashion-beauty`)

- "Trị", "điều trị", "chữa trị" (treat, treatment, cure; e.g. trị mụn = treat acne, trị nám = treat melasma) are not accepted in cosmetic claims or names [L]
  (CV 1609/QLD-MP; ASEAN Cosmetic Claim Guidelines). No "trắng da cấp tốc" (whiten skin fast), "mọc tóc" (grow hair).
- No filters that change the result; the same grade on every shot [P: misleading staging].
- Before/after barred for acne, whitening, hair loss, and in all ads (§3).

## 7. Finance, health, legal advice (`talking-head`)

- Finance ads on TikTok in Vietnam (banking, credit, investment) only from advertisers registered with
  SBV, SSC or MOF; get-rich-quick and too-good-to-be-true offers banned everywhere [P].
- Advice is hedged; no guaranteed outcomes; credentials shown are real; numbers have a source.

## 8. Promotions (`promo-sale`)

The old price was real; dates and conditions match the promotion (registered "khuyến mại", i.e. promotions, where required: ask); no invented countdown; voucher codes exact [L/C].
