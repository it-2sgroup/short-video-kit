# Platform Specs and Commercial Content Policy

Read this **before** writing voiceover or on-screen text for any product video. For a cleaning,
laundry or personal-care product, most of the moderation risk lives in the words, not the footage —
so it is cheaper to get the script right than to re-shoot.

Sources are marked by strength:
- **[P]** TikTok or FTC documentation. Authoritative; act on it.
- **[C]** Craft convention or creator consensus. Sensible; not binding.

## 1. Prohibited claims

These apply directly to household cleaning, laundry and stain-removal products.

| Prohibited | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Antibacterial / antimicrobial claims** | Cleaning and rinse products may **not** claim to kill, remove or inhibit the growth of bacteria, fungi or viruses. TikTok's stated acceptable alternative is describing cleaning of dirt while *reducing the accumulation* of bacteria. | [P] seller-th.tiktok.com `knowledge_id=4924400659187458` |
| **Instant-effect claims** | "See results instantly", "instant removal", "instant restoration" are enumerated exaggerated promises. | [P] same |
| **Exaggerated cleaning / restoration claims** | Claims that a product instantly removes stains, or that a fabric product instantly restores colour, are named almost verbatim for this category. | [P] seller-us.tiktok.com `knowledge_id=3196690250417921` |
| **"Ends all problems" framing** | Any claim that the product ends an entire category of problem. "Removes any stain" / "sạch mọi vết bẩn" is in scope. | [P] seller-th |
| **Miracle / magic / secret formula** | "Secret formula", "breakthrough", "magical", "new discovery". | [P] seller-th |
| **Absolute superlatives** | "Best", "number one", "the only", "the first", "market leader" without objective verifiable evidence. | [P] seller-uk.tiktok.com `knowledge_id=5434012677392150` |
| **Guaranteed results / money-back** | Falls under exaggerated promises and unverifiable results. Promotional terms stated in content must match the listing. | [P] ads.tiktok.com misleading-content policy |
| **Competitor comparison** | Content must not compare against other products or sellers, or claim to be cheaper than competitors. | [P] seller-th |
| **Malicious comparison** | Accusing competitors of violations, or subjective negative judgements of their core attributes ("other detergents wreck your clothes"). | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| **False scarcity / urgency** | Unverifiable urgency ("only 3 left") unless real and reflected on the listing. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |

**The one permitted superlative.** Claims about *your own* sales are explicitly allowed — "my
top-selling product on TikTok Shop" is fine. This matters because social-proof framing is both
compliant and, in the one hook dataset with disclosed methodology, the best-performing archetype.
It is the rare case where the safe option is also the strong one.

### Scripts already in circulation that need rewriting

The existing creator brief for this campaign contains several lines that fall foul of the rules
above. If you are adapting an existing script, check for these:

| Existing line | Problem |
|---|---|
| "Không hiệu quả anh gửi tiền lại liền" | Money-back guarantee stated in content. |
| "Hổng sạch hổng lấy tiền" | Same — guaranteed-results claim. |
| "Nói dóc làm chó, cái này mà không tẩy được áo ố thì anh nghỉ bán luôn" | Guarantee framing by implication. |
| "sạch tinh như mới" / "trắng bóc như mới" | Restoration claim; borderline with "instant". |
| "tẩy sạch mọi vết bẩn cứng đầu" | "Ends all problems" framing. |

These are not hypothetical — the money-back line appears on one of the campaign's highest-earning
videos. Performing well is not evidence of compliance; it is evidence that enforcement had not
caught up. Do not propagate it into new scripts, and say so plainly if asked to.

## 2. Before/after demonstration rules

Before/after is **permitted** for cleaning products (unlike acne, whitening and anti-hair-loss
products, where it is banned outright in listings). But it is regulated on three axes.

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No false impression | Before/after depictions creating a false impression of outcomes are prohibited under "problematic comparisons". | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| No unrealistic transformation | Named example: wrinkles disappearing from cream use. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| Consistent lighting, angle, editing | Misleading background and staging are named as misleading claims; before/after using different lighting, angles or editing is prohibited. | [P] seller-us `knowledge_id=4581457528194817` |
| Demonstrations must reflect real performance | A demonstration must accurately reflect performance under normal consumer use. | [P] FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance |
| Results must be typical or disclosed | Before/after must represent typical consumer experience, or carry a clear and conspicuous disclosure of generally expected results. | [P] FTC Endorsement Guides |
| No artificial enhancement | Enforcement precedent includes hidden wires enhancing a toy's apparent performance. | [P] FTC |
| AI-generated demos | Demos showing instant or unrealistic effects via AI are prohibited; substantially AI-edited content must be disclosed. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |

**The useful insight:** the things that make a demo *believable* to a viewer are the same things
that make it *compliant*. An unbroken take, a locked frame, constant lighting, visible hands, a
control half left untreated, and an honestly imperfect final result all serve both goals at once.
Build the shot for believability and compliance follows. Build it for drama and you fail both.

**Time compression must be labelled.** If footage is sped up, put it on screen — "tua nhanh ×8",
"sau 2 tiếng ngâm". This satisfies both TikTok's instant-effect rule and the FTC's demonstration
guidance, and costs you nothing.

## 3. Disclosure requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial disclosure** | Mandatory whenever anything of value changed hands. Turn on "Disclose commercial content" → Branded Content before publishing. Undisclosed commercial content faces visibility reduction or forced labelling; repeat violations escalate to posting restrictions or ban. | [P] tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/regulated-commercial-activities |
| **AI-generated content** | Substantially AI-edited content must be disclosed via label, disclaimer, caption, watermark or sticker. Undisclosed AI content is rejected or restricted. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |

Both of these are settings the publisher toggles, not something the edit can fix. Include them as
a line item in every delivery checklist so they are not forgotten at upload time.

## 4. What gets a video demoted or removed

| Trigger | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Unoriginal / reused content | Reused content without creative edit, or carrying another platform's watermark, is **ineligible for the For You feed** and for Creator Rewards. | [P-adjacent] tiktok.com/creator-academy originality policy |
| Script reuse | TikTok Shop creator policy requires an original script per video. | [P-adjacent] seller-us `knowledge_id=4984474064766737` |
| Fake interactive elements | Fake play buttons, close buttons, CTA buttons, non-functional carousel indicators. **Never draw a fake cart or fake "buy" button** — point at the real one. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| Content / listing mismatch | Promotions, pricing or discounts stated in video must match the destination listing. | [P] ads.tiktok.com |
| Evasion tactics | Special characters or abbreviations used to hide banned terms; edited video used to evade enforcement. | [P-adjacent] |
| Removed videos still count | A shoppable video removed for violation still counts toward posting frequency limits. | [P-adjacent] seller-us `knowledge_id=7608640301074219` |
| Penalty ladder | Warning → content/product removal → violation points → privilege suspension → permanent deactivation, plus reduced algorithmic visibility. | [P] seller-th |
| Vietnam market | Same misleading-content policy applies; TikTok Shop VN removes the product *and* related content on detection. | [P] seller-vn `knowledge_id=6837773789480706` |

**The script-reuse rule deserves attention.** A campaign that distributes one script to twenty
creators is, on its face, in tension with the original-script requirement. When adapting a brief
with numbered scripts, change the opening line, the specific stains named, and the personal framing
for each creator rather than handing out a verbatim copy.

## 5. Music licensing

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial Music Library** | Business accounts must use TikTok's Commercial Music Library, not the general consumer music catalogue. Using chart music on a commercial video is a licensing violation. | [P-adjacent] |
| What compliant affiliates actually do | In a study of 1,842 TikTok Shop affiliate videos across 67 brands, 1,360 (≈74%) used original audio or no music; 412 used the Commercial Music Library. | [D] oktorun.ai/research/tiktok-shop-affiliate-music-study |

**A correction worth knowing.** The widely-repeated claim that *"trending audio generated 58% more
add-to-cart actions"* is misattributed. The study it is credited to measured music licensing
compliance only and explicitly did not measure conversions. Do not repeat the statistic, and do not
let it drive a decision to risk a licensing violation.

**Defensible default:** original voiceover as the primary audio, with a cleared library track low
in the bed. See `audio-and-music.md` for levels.

## 6. Technical specs and safe zones

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical) |
| Frame rate | 30 fps |
| Duration target | 30–40s ideal; 25–45s acceptable. See `niche-winning-patterns.md` §2 for the evidence. |
| Shoppable video minimum | 8s (TikTok Shop creator eligibility) |
| Creator Rewards ceiling | Shoppable videos ≥60s are disqualified from Creator Rewards (commission still paid) |

**Safe zones for 1080×1920.** Published figures disagree (TikTok publishes safe-area templates for
ads, not organic), so design to the most conservative overlap:

| Edge | Keep clear | What occupies it |
|---|---|---|
| Top | 250 px | Status bar, following/for-you tabs |
| Bottom | 510 px | Username, caption, audio tag, product card |
| Right | 140 px | Like / comment / share / cart rail |
| Left | 60 px | General margin |

That leaves a usable band roughly **y = 250 → 1410**, **x = 60 → 940**. Put captions and key text
inside it. A caption that looks fine in your preview and is covered by the app's own chrome on a
real phone is the most common avoidable delivery defect.

## 7. Rewrite table

Use this when adapting existing scripts. The left column is what people write; the right column
keeps the persuasive force without the prohibited claim.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Diệt khuẩn 99.9%" | "Làm sạch bụi bẩn bám trên vải" |
| "Tẩy sạch ngay lập tức" | "Ngâm 2 tiếng là thấy khác rõ" |
| "Sạch mọi vết bẩn" | "Mình thử với vết thâm kim, ố vàng và dầu mỡ" |
| "Trắng bóc như mới" | "Sáng hơn hẳn so với lúc đầu" |
| "Không sạch hoàn tiền" | *(remove entirely — no compliant rewrite)* |
| "Tốt nhất thị trường" | "Sản phẩm bán chạy nhất shop mình trên TikTok Shop" |
| "Hơn hẳn mấy loại khác" | "So với cách mình giặt tay trước đây thì đỡ công hơn" |
| "Công thức bí mật" | "Công thức OXI hoạt tính" |
| "Chỉ còn 3 suất cuối" | *(remove unless the listing really shows limited stock)* |

Note the pattern in the compliant column: it replaces absolute claims with **specific, bounded,
first-person observations**. That is not just safer — specificity is also what the caption data in
`niche-winning-patterns.md` §5 associates with better-performing videos. The compliant phrasing and
the effective phrasing point the same direction.
